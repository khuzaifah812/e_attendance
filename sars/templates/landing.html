Everything attendance needs, in one system

From the lecture room to the semester calendar, SARS keeps students, lecturers and administrators in sync.

{% icon 'camera' 26 %}

Face Recognition & QR Code

Students prove they are present by face scan or by the QR code the lecturer generates when the session is activated.

{% icon 'pin' 26 %}

Room Location Verification

Physical-lecture attendance only works when the student is physically inside the lecture room, checked by GPS.

{% icon 'clock' 26 %}

30-Minute Online Codes

Online lectures use a secure code that lasts 30 minutes and is only valid during the lecture time. One attendance per lecture.

{% icon 'trending' 26 %}

Live Attendance Percentage

Calculated from lectures actually taught in the semester, emergency lectures included, per course and overall.

{% icon 'bell' 26 %}

Smart Reminders

Students are reminded 5 hours, 1 hour and 10 minutes before a lecture; lecturers 2 hours and 10 minutes. By email and in the system.

{% icon 'calendar' 26 %}

Semester Calendar

Every activity from day one to the end of the semester: lectures, coursework, exams, meetings and emergency lectures.

{% icon 'send' 26 %}

Announcements & Coursework

Lecturers post announcements, notes and assignments that reach all their students on the system and by email.

{% icon 'shield' 26 %}

Role-Based Dashboards

Dedicated dashboards for students, lecturers, coordinators and the administrator who controls everything.

{% icon 'printer' 26 %}

Printable Attendance Lists

Print a complete attendance list with the student's details, course summaries and every lecture attended.

How it works

Four simple steps from registration to results.

Register

Create your account. Your username is your registration number and your password is your name.

Join courses

Register for the courses of your level and see your timetable and calendar.

Take attendance

When the lecturer activates the session, verify your location and face or enter the code.

Track progress

Watch your attendance percentage grow and print your full attendance list.

One system, four roles

Each person sees exactly what they need.

Student

Attendance, percentage, courses, calendar, notifications, coursework and printing.

Lecturer

Timetable without clashes, session codes & QR, live monitoring, announcements and notes.

Coordinator

All lecturer tools plus managing students, courses, rooms and semester activities.

Administrator

Controls everything and can open any student's dashboard with one click.
